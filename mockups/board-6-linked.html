Everything links to a goal or to maintenance — the colored chips. Now the bottom scoreboard answers the real question: am I actually moving my 2026 goals, or just doing life maintenance? "Side project" is visibly behind because the week filled up — and the app says so instead of hiding it.

Two new bits since the last mock: (1) crossing off a rolling item pops the orange "next step?" suggestion you confirm or edit (see Vacation). (2) tiny one-offs don't each grab a calendar block — they pool into a single ⚡ Knock-out window so the day stays clean. Goals + a maintenance bucket are the only organizing layer — no separate "priorities" list to manage.
