This is your whiteboard, exactly. Three regions: your 2026 Goals (north star), Up next (the rolling bullets — cross one off and it asks for the next step, just like erasing "buy tickets" and writing "plan itinerary"), and This week, the only region that knows your work calendar.

In region 3: 🔒 work blocks come from your read-only Outlook feed; priority blocks get suggested into the gaps and nothing moves until you tap Add it (suggest-and-approve). Some priorities are flexible ("deep work: 10 hrs, fit anywhere"), some have windows ("gym = mornings"). The honest scoreboard tells you what won't fit.

And the whole point — checking things off — is everywhere, with the satisfying strike. Unlike the whiteboard, nothing you write ever falls off or gets forgotten.
