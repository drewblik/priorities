Fit — the whole app is one job: take your work calendar as fixed (the 🔒 Outlook blocks) and slot your priority time-blocks into the gaps. When work changes — like the orange 2:00 sync that just appeared — it re-fits automatically and shows you exactly what moved, with one tap to keep or undo. The "This week" card is the honest scoreboard: what's landing, and what won't fit so you can make the call.

Gone vs. today's app: council of chatbots, Quarter/Week/Day planning rituals, queues, onboarding coach, master-chat router. Priorities are just a name + a weekly time target (e.g. "Deep work: 10 hrs", "Gym: 3×"). The calendar engine is the product.
