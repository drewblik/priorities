Whiteboard + Sticky Notes — your blend of ① and ②. Each thing lives on its own sticky you can scribble on; the week + meals are structured zones; there's a freeform Dump sticky for whatever doesn't fit yet. Priorities are sticky tabs along the top (Health, Career, …) — tangible and glanceable, not a database you manage. The dashed orange note is the only "AI" — it quietly pins what you keep forgetting to the top and never wipes the board.

Imagine: tap a sticky to edit it, tap + to add a new one, drag to rearrange. Tap a priority tab to dim everything except its notes.
